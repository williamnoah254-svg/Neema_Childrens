Ways to support Neema

Help make care possible, every day.

Neema Children's Centre supports vulnerable children in Mombasa with a safe home, education and guidance. Practical, thoughtful support helps us keep that care going.

Current priorities

Support the essentials

Children's needs change throughout the year. These are areas where consistent support helps sustain daily care. Contact us for the latest priorities before arranging an in-kind donation.

  • Education

    School fees and learning materials

    Fees, uniforms, books and stationery help children attend school and take part in learning.

  • Daily care

    Food and nutrition

    Balanced meals support children's health, growth and ability to learn.

  • Safe home

    Bedding and clothing

    Mattresses, blankets, sheets and well-fitting clothes help make the home comfortable and dignified.

  • Health

    Medical care

    Check-ups, treatment, medicine and referrals help children get the care they need.

  • Future skills

    Vocational training equipment

    Tools such as sewing machines and computers help young people build practical skills.

  • Time and care

    Mentors and tutors

    Consistent, vetted volunteers can support learning, confidence and personal development.

Offer your time and skills

Volunteer with care

We welcome people who can offer reliable, respectful support. Roles are agreed with our team and depend on current needs, availability and safeguarding approval.

  • Mentorship

    Provide steady encouragement and guidance to a young person.

    Monthly · minimum one year

  • Tutoring and homework

    Help with schoolwork, literacy and exam preparation.

    Weekly · around two hours

  • Skills training

    Share practical experience in areas such as tailoring, computing, crafts or business.

    Flexible · project-based

  • Professional services

    Offer relevant expertise in health, law, finance, communications or construction.

    Project-based

  • Events and group support

    Help with approved fundraising, resource drives and community activities.

    Scheduled · by arrangement

Child safety comes first

All volunteers complete our vetting process, including identification, references and a code of conduct. Child-facing activities begin only after approval.
