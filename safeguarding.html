Safeguarding and Privacy

Our commitment to protecting every child in our care, and to handling your personal information with care.

Our Commitment

Neema Children's Home is a community-based sanctuary located in Mikajuni, Jomvu Sub-county, Mombasa, Kenya. The safety and wellbeing of every child in our care is our highest priority. We apply a zero-tolerance approach to abuse, neglect, and exploitation in any form.

This page explains how we protect children and how we handle personal information. It applies to everyone who engages with Neema, including staff, volunteers, visitors, partners, and donors.

Safeguarding Commitment

Zero Tolerance for Abuse

We do not tolerate any form of abuse, neglect, exploitation, or harm toward children. This applies to every person associated with Neema, without exception.

Our Safeguarding Policy

Our full safeguarding policy is reviewed regularly and covers:

  • Recruitment and vetting of all staff and volunteers
  • Code of conduct for adults working with children
  • Reporting and response procedures for concerns
  • Training for all staff and volunteers
  • Confidential handling of all reports
  • Cooperation with local authorities and statutory bodies

To request a copy of our full safeguarding policy, contact our designated safeguarding lead using the details in the section below.

Staff and Volunteer Vetting

Every person who works with the children at Neema is vetted before any contact. Vetting includes:

  • Verification of identification
  • Reference checks
  • Police clearance or equivalent where required
  • Signed code of conduct
  • Safeguarding induction training

Vetting is repeated periodically, and any concerns result in immediate action.

Safe Visiting and Photography Rules

Visiting the Home

All visits are by prior arrangement only. We do not accept walk-in visitors. This protects the children and ensures that every visitor is known, vetted, and supervised.

  1. Request a visit in advance through our official channels.
  2. Provide identification and purpose of visit.
  3. Wait for written approval from our team.
  4. Sign in on arrival and follow staff guidance at all times.
  5. Do not have unsupervised contact with any child.
  6. Follow our photography rules below.

Photography and Media

To protect the children's privacy and dignity, the following rules apply to all visitors, volunteers, and partners:

  • Do not photograph or film any child without written consent from our management.
  • Do not post images or videos of children on personal social media.
  • Do not publish any identifying details, including names, ages, or locations, without approval.
  • All official photography is managed by our team and follows our consent procedures.
  • Any photography that identifies a child must have prior written consent from the child's guardian and our management.

Where a child's photograph is used with consent, faces may be obscured, names changed, or images illustrated to protect identity. Any breach of these rules may lead to the immediate end of a visit or relationship with Neema.

Report a Concern

If a Child Is in Immediate Danger

If a child is in immediate danger, contact the local emergency services or the relevant child protection authority in Kenya right away.

Emergency services: 999 or 112

After alerting the authorities, please also inform our safeguarding lead so we can respond and support.

How to Report a Concern

Anyone can report a concern, including staff, volunteers, visitors, partners, donors, and members of the community. You do not need to be certain that harm has occurred. If something concerns you, report it.

What Happens When You Report

  1. Your report is received by the designated safeguarding lead.
  2. The concern is assessed promptly and confidentially.
  3. Where needed, we involve the relevant authorities.
  4. We take protective action to keep the child safe.
  5. We follow up with the reporter where appropriate, respecting confidentiality.

Confidentiality

We treat all reports confidentially. Information is shared only with those who need to know in order to protect the child and respond appropriately. We will not disclose your identity without your consent unless required by law.

Protection for Reporters

No one who reports a concern in good faith will face any negative consequences. We are committed to a culture where speaking up is encouraged and supported.

Privacy Notice

Last updated: [insert date]

This Privacy Notice explains how Neema Children's Home collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information. It applies to our website, contact forms, donation processes, and any other interaction you have with us.

Who We Are

Neema Children's Home is a community-based sanctuary in Mikajuni, Jomvu Sub-county, Mombasa, Kenya. For privacy matters, you can contact us at neemachildrencentermombasa@gmail.com.

What Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to serve our mission and respond to you. This may include:

  • Contact details you provide through our forms, such as name, email, phone number, and message.
  • Donation information, including name, contact details, donation amount, and payment reference.
  • Volunteer or partnership enquiry details, including the information you choose to share about your background or organisation.
  • Basic technical data such as browser type and pages visited, if analytics are enabled with your consent.
  • Cookies and similar technologies, subject to your consent.

How We Use Your Information

We use personal information to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and requests.
  • Process and acknowledge donations.
  • Manage volunteering, partnership, and visiting arrangements.
  • Send updates and news, if you have opted in.
  • Meet legal, accounting, and regulatory obligations.
  • Protect children and comply with safeguarding procedures.
  • Improve our website and services.

Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal information based on one or more of the following:

  • Your consent, when you submit a form, opt in to communications, or accept cookies.
  • Performance of a contract or arrangement, such as processing a donation or volunteer application.
  • Legal obligations, such as financial records and safeguarding compliance.
  • Legitimate interests, such as responding to enquiries and protecting the children in our care.

Who We Share Information With

We do not sell personal information. We may share it only with:

  • Payment processors and financial institutions, to process donations.
  • Service providers who help us operate the website, subject to confidentiality agreements.
  • Regulatory or statutory authorities, where required by law.
  • Child protection or law enforcement bodies, where necessary to protect a child.

How Long We Keep Information

We keep personal information only as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected. As a guide:

  • Enquiries and general correspondence: up to 24 months, then deleted.
  • Donation records: up to seven years, to meet financial and legal requirements.
  • Volunteer records: for the duration of the relationship plus a reasonable period afterward.
  • Safeguarding records: retained as required by law and best practice.
  • Website analytics: retained according to the analytics provider's settings, usually up to 14 months.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your information, subject to legal requirements.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where consent is the basis for processing.
  • Lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at neemachildrencentermombasa@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies for essential functionality, analytics, and improving your experience. Where required, we ask for your consent before setting non-essential cookies. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. These include secure connections, access controls, and staff training. No system is completely secure, but we work to keep your information safe.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the website. Information about the children in our care is handled in line with our safeguarding policy and applicable law.

Changes to This Notice

We may update this Privacy Notice from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last revised. Continued use of our website or services after changes means you accept the updated notice.
