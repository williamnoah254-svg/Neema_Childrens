About Neema Children's Centre

A community-based sanctuary providing holistic care for vulnerable children in Mikajuni, Jomvu Sub-county, Mombasa.

Who We Are

Neema Children's Centre is a community-based sanctuary in Mikajuni, Jomvu Sub-county, Mombasa. Since 2012, we have provided safety, care, education, and hope to vulnerable children in our community, many of whom have been affected by loss, hardship, and the HIV/AIDS epidemic.

Today we house 46 children and run an outreach programme that supports dozens of vulnerable families in the surrounding neighbourhood. Our work is rooted in the belief that every child deserves to grow up safe, loved, and equipped for the future.

Who We Serve

Neema Children's Centre currently houses 46 children: 23 girls, 19 boys, and infants in our care. We welcome children aged 3 to 16, from both Christian and Muslim families, and we respect and nurture the faith background of every child.

Beyond the children who live at the centre, we run an outreach programme that extends support to dozens of vulnerable families in the surrounding Mikajuni neighbourhood. This outreach allows us to reach children at risk of losing family care, and to strengthen households so that families can stay together.

Our History

Neema Children's Centre was established in 2012 by the late Mrs. Mulongo Chondo Wadio, who served as founder and director. She dedicated her life to caring for orphans and vulnerable children, beginning with 60 children — 25 boys and 35 girls. Mr. Omar Kalama served as her assistant, supporting the home's work and daily activities.

2025, the late Mrs. Mulongo Chondo Wadio passed away, leaving behind a legacy of compassion, dedication, and love for vulnerable children. Following her passing, her daughter, Ms. Halima Kahoso, became the director of Neema Children's Home, continuing her mother's vision and commitment to caring for children in need.

Neema Children's Home supports 40 orphans and vulnerable children in Mombasa, Kenya. Under the current leadership, the home remains committed to promoting children's well-being, supporting their education, and creating opportunities for a brighter future.

Neema Children's Home welcomes partnerships with companies, organizations, individuals, and well-wishers who share its commitment to improving the lives of vulnerable children.

Our vision is to ensure that every child receives love, care, protection, and the opportunity to reach their full potential.

Our Mission

The mission of Neema Children's Home is to provide orphans and vulnerable children with access to quality education, safe and comfortable shelter, good health care, love, protection, and proper care. We are committed to creating a safe, nurturing, and supportive environment where every child feels valued, develops their potential, and has the opportunity to build a brighter future.

Through dedicated care and support, we aim to help every child at Neema Children's Home achieve their dreams and build a brighter future.

Our Vision

A society where every orphan and vulnerable child has access to quality education, proper health care, safe shelter, and equal opportunities to achieve their dreams.

We aspire to raise children who are confident, responsible, self-reliant, and capable of contributing positively to society.

Our Values

Six principles guide how we care for children, work with families, and engage with our community.

Dignity

We treat every child, family member, and partner with respect and honour, recognising the inherent worth of each person.

Family

We believe children thrive best in families, and we work to preserve, strengthen, and restore family connections wherever it is safe to do so.

Integrity

We are transparent, accountable, and responsible in all that we do, and we handle every resource entrusted to us with care.

Compassion

We serve with empathy, patience, and genuine care for each child's wellbeing, meeting children where they are and walking with them at their pace.

Empowerment

We equip children and families with the skills, knowledge, and confidence they need to build independent, stable futures.

Protection

We are committed to the safety and safeguarding of every child in our care, and we apply a zero-tolerance approach to any form of harm.

Leadership

Neema Children's Centre is led by Ms. Kahoso, who serves as the director. She is supported by a management committee, a social worker, a caregiver, and a cook. Together, they oversee the daily operations of the home and ensure that 40 orphans and vulnerable children receive proper care, education, health care, protection, and emotional support.
