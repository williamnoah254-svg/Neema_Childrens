Report a Concern

If you are worried about the safety or wellbeing of any child at Neema Children's Home, please tell us. All reports are treated confidentially and taken seriously.

Speak Up. It Matters.

Anyone can report a concern: staff, volunteers, visitors, partners, donors, neighbours, and members of the community. You do not need to be certain that harm has occurred. If something worries you, share it with us.

We take every report seriously and respond quickly. Our priority is the safety of the child, and we will take the protective action needed.

If a Child Is in Immediate Danger

Do not wait. Contact emergency services right away, then inform our designated safeguarding lead.

Emergency services in Kenya: 999 or 112

Child protection authorities and local police can also act immediately. Once the child is safe, please share the details with our safeguarding lead so we can respond internally.

What You Can Report

You can report anything that concerns you about a child's safety, dignity, or wellbeing. You do not need to prove anything or have all the facts.

Examples of Concerns

  • Physical, emotional, or sexual abuse of a child
  • Neglect of a child's basic needs
  • Unsafe or inappropriate behaviour by an adult toward a child
  • Violation of our safeguarding policy or code of conduct
  • Concerns about a child's mental health or wellbeing
  • Concerns about a visitor, volunteer, or staff member
  • Online or digital safety concerns involving a child
  • Any other situation that makes you worried about a child

What We Need From You

When you report, share as much as you can remember: what happened, when, where, and who was involved. If you do not know, that is fine. Share what you do know, and we will follow up from there.

What Happens After You Report

Every report follows the same process. Our priority at every step is the safety of the child.

  1. Your report is received by the designated safeguarding lead and reviewed promptly.
  2. The concern is assessed on the same day or, in urgent cases, immediately.
  3. We take protective action to keep the child safe, including involving the authorities where needed.
  4. We coordinate with local child protection services, police, or medical professionals as appropriate.
  5. We follow up with you where possible, respecting confidentiality and your preferences.
  6. We record the concern and our response, in line with our safeguarding policy and legal requirements.

Confidentiality and Protection for Reporters

Your Privacy Is Protected

All reports are treated confidentially. Information is shared only with those who need to know in order to protect the child and respond appropriately. We will not disclose your identity without your consent unless required by law.

No one who reports a concern in good faith will face any negative consequences. We are committed to a culture where speaking up is encouraged, supported, and respected.

If you have questions about how we handle reports, contact us at neemachildrencentermombasa@gmail.com or read our Safeguarding and Privacy page.

Thank You for Speaking Up

Every report helps us protect the children in our care. Your courage makes a difference.
