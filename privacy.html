1. Who We Are

Neema Children's Home is a community-based sanctuary located in Mikajuni, Jomvu Sub-county, Mombasa, Kenya. We provide holistic care for vulnerable children, and we support at-risk families in the surrounding community.

For the purposes of this notice, Neema Children's Home is the data controller responsible for the personal information described here. You can reach us at neemachildrencentermombasa@gmail.com for any privacy matter.

2. What Information We Collect

We collect only the information we need to serve our mission, respond to enquiries, and meet legal obligations. Depending on how you interact with us, this may include:

  • Contact details you provide through our forms, such as name, email address, phone number, and message content.
  • Donation information, including your name, contact details, donation amount, date, payment reference, and designation.
  • Volunteer or partnership enquiry details, including information about your background, skills, or organisation.
  • Safeguarding report details, including any information you choose to share about a concern.
  • Basic technical data such as browser type, device type, approximate location, and pages visited, if analytics are enabled with your consent.
  • Cookies and similar technologies, subject to your consent where required.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information only for the purposes for which it was provided, and for related purposes that you would reasonably expect. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Respond to your enquiries, requests, and messages.
  • Process and acknowledge donations.
  • Maintain donation and financial records as required by law.
  • Manage volunteering, partnership, and visiting arrangements.
  • Send news, updates, and appeals, if you have opted in to receive them.
  • Protect children and comply with safeguarding procedures.
  • Meet legal, accounting, and regulatory obligations.
  • Improve our website, services, and communications.

We will not use your information for purposes unrelated to these without your consent.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal information on one or more of the following bases:

  • Consent. When you submit a form, opt in to communications, accept cookies, or provide information for a specific purpose, you give us permission to process it.
  • Contract or arrangement. When processing is necessary to fulfil a request, such as handling a donation or volunteer application.
  • Legal obligation. When processing is required to comply with applicable laws, such as financial record-keeping, tax, or safeguarding duties.
  • Legitimate interests. When processing is necessary for our legitimate interests, such as responding to enquiries and protecting the children in our care, provided those interests do not override your rights.

5. Who We Share Information With

We do not sell personal information. We share it only where necessary and with appropriate safeguards. This may include:

  • Payment processors and financial institutions, to process donations and issue receipts.
  • Service providers who help us operate the website and communications, under confidentiality agreements.
  • Professional advisors such as accountants, auditors, and legal counsel, where required.
  • Regulatory or statutory authorities, where required by law.
  • Child protection services, police, or medical professionals, where necessary to protect a child.

Where we share information with service providers, we require them to handle it in line with this notice and applicable law.

6. How Long We Keep Information

We keep personal information only as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, and to meet legal obligations. As a guide:

  • Enquiries and general correspondence: up to 24 months, after which they are deleted.
  • Donation records: up to 7 years, to meet financial and legal requirements.
  • Volunteer and partnership records: for the duration of the relationship plus a reasonable period afterward.
  • Safeguarding records: retained as required by law and best practice, and handled with the highest confidentiality.
  • Website analytics: retained according to the analytics provider's settings, typically up to 14 months.
  • Mailing list subscriptions: kept until you unsubscribe or request removal.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access. You can request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction. You can ask us to correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Deletion. You can request that we delete your information, subject to legal requirements.
  • Restriction. You can ask us to limit how we use your information in certain circumstances.
  • Objection. You can object to certain types of processing, including direct marketing.
  • Withdraw consent. Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it at any time.
  • Complaint. You can lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority, including the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner in Kenya.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at neemachildrencentermombasa@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time and in line with applicable law.

8. Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies for essential functionality, analytics, and improving your experience. Where required by law, we ask for your consent before setting non-essential cookies.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect how parts of the website function.

If we use analytics, we choose privacy-respecting tools where possible and only track data with your consent. Analytics data is used to understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it.

9. Security

We take reasonable and appropriate measures to protect personal information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or alteration. These measures include:

  • Secure connections (HTTPS) on our website.
  • Access controls, so that only authorised staff can access personal information.
  • Staff training on data protection and confidentiality.
  • Regular review of our systems and processes.

No system is completely secure. If you believe your information has been compromised, contact us immediately at neemachildrencentermombasa@gmail.com.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the website. Any information about the children in our care is handled in line with our safeguarding policy, applicable child protection laws, and our commitment to protecting their privacy and dignity.

Photographs, stories, and other identifying information about children are only published with proper consent and in line with our safeguarding policy.

11. Changes to This Notice

We may update this Privacy Notice from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or the law. When we make significant changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, provide additional notice.

Continued use of our website or services after changes take effect means you accept the updated notice. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
