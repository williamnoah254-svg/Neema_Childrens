What Happens Next

Your gift is already at work. Here is what you can expect from us in the coming days and weeks.

Official Receipt

You will receive an official receipt by email. Donations made before the 25th of the month are receipted within the same month.

Confirmation of Use

If you designated your gift to a programme, it will be applied there. If a programme is fully funded, we will inform you how we redirected it.
