Who We Are

Neema Children's Centre is based in Mombasa, Kenya, and is dedicated to caring for orphans and vulnerable children. We strive to provide a safe and supportive environment where children can access education, health care, guidance, and opportunities to develop their potential. Together with our partners, well-wishers, and volunteers, we hope to build a brighter future for every child.

Our Programmes

We provide holistic care through seven core programmes.

Education

School support, uniforms, fees, tutoring, and smooth transitions between levels.

Health

Routine check-ups, referrals, nutrition, hygiene, and partnerships with medical providers.

Counselling

Trauma support, individual and group sessions, and clear referral pathways.

Mentorship

One-on-one mentors, life skills, and positive role models for every child.

Skills Development

Vocational training, tailoring, IT, arts, and entrepreneurship for older children.

Family Strengthening

Support to at-risk families through parenting guidance and economic empowerment.

Reintegration

Family tracing, preparation, follow-up, and aftercare for children returning home.
