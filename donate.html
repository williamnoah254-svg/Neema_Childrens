Your gift provides a safe home, an education, and a future for a child in Mikajuni, Jomvu Sub-county, Mombasa.

Why Your Gift Matters

Every child at Neema Children's Home carries a story of resilience. Many have experienced loss, displacement, or hardship. Your donation provides shelter, meals, education, healthcare, and the emotional support that children need to heal and grow.

NOTE:

For financial contributions, please contact the director to obtain the appropriate M-Pesa or bank account details..

Every Gift Changes a Life

Whether you give once or every month, you become part of a child's story of hope.
